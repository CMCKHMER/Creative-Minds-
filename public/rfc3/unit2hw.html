📖 Reading passage — Leonardo da Vinci (210 words) · tap to open

Leonardo da Vinci was born on April 15th, 1452. He grew up in a town called Vinci, in Italy. Interestingly, his name means "Leonardo from Vinci." When he was a student, his teachers were often very happy with his work. People called him a genius because he was excellent at whatever he did.

He is most famous for his paintings. Many people think da Vinci is one of the greatest painters who ever lived. Two of his paintings, the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper, are still famous today. The Mona Lisa is kept in the Louvre Museum in Paris, France. Every day, thousands of visitors line up to see it.

However, da Vinci's talent was not limited to painting. He was also talented in science. Da Vinci was an engineer and liked to make things. Surprisingly, he thought of many things that we use today. For example, in his notebooks, he wrote about things like helicopters and army tanks. He also thought about solar power and calculators. He imagined these things in the 1500s. This was a few hundred years before people actually used them.

Da Vinci died in France on May 2nd, 1519. Today, only a few of his paintings and notebooks remain. They will always be treasures.
