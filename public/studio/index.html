About Creative Minds Studio

Creative Minds Studio is a browser-based creative editor combining vector drawing, photo editing, rich-text documents (Word-style), spreadsheets (Excel-style), layers with blend modes, image warping, background removal, and PDF export — all in one self-contained HTML file. No login, no server, no telemetry.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your work stays on your device. Auto-saves to localStorage every 30s.

Layers: 16 blend modes, per-layer opacity, visibility, lock, merge down, flatten.

Page sizes: A4, A3, Letter, Legal, Square, HD, Story, Banner, Custom. Plus orientation flip.

Image warping: Free Transform, Perspective Warp, Flip H/V, Rotate 90° CW/CCW/180°, Rotate Image 90°, Rotate Document 15°.

Background removal: chroma-key (green-screen) and magic-wand (color threshold) with edge refinement.

Photo filters: brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, sepia, grayscale, hue, plus presets (B&W, Vintage, Vivid, Warm, Cool) and effects (grain, vignette, dust, halation).

Fonts: 14 popular Google fonts (Inter, Roboto, Lato, Montserrat, Playfair Display, Merriweather, Poppins, Raleway, Source Sans 3, Oswald, Syne, Roboto Mono) + Georgia, Courier New.

Spreadsheets: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, ROUND, ABS, arithmetic, cell refs.

Export: PNG, JPG, SVG (vector), PDF (single-page, vector + rasterized text), JSON (full project), Print.
