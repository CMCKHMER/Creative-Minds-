Button Lab — six candidates

Your buttons already have a hard 3D base edge (0 4px 0 #0e7490) and a travelling sheen, so the site is not flat — it is quiet. The gap is that almost all the life is in hover, which a phone never fires. Each idea below is ranked by how much “alive” it buys per unit of cost, and every one animates only transform / opacity. Tap the buttons; they are real.

Resize / use your phone: hover-only effects disappear on touch by design.

The light theme is the real test

Your html.light block already re-states dozens of dark-theme effects. A 3D edge needs its own darker base colour here (#0b6b82 works on the pale surface, the glow has to drop to ~35% alpha), and the glare must switch from white to a cyan tint or it disappears. Any candidate that cannot survive this strip is not worth wiring in.
